# LeetCode flashcards

LeetCode flashcards is a practice site for your phone. You use it on your commute to practise LeetCode patterns. Each card shows a problem, and you recall the interview steps before you reveal them. Cards come back on a schedule that follows your ratings.

## Features

- [Every page](every-page.md). The rules that apply on all pages: phone size, no signal, home screen, progress on all your devices.
- [Practice session](practice-session.md). You review the cards that are due today, then a small number of new cards.
- [Flashcard](flashcard.md). You read a problem, commit to each step in your head, and reveal the steps one at a time.
- [Pattern decision tree](pattern-decision-tree.md). You answer yes or no questions about a problem to find its pattern.
- [Patterns page](patterns-page.md). You read each pattern, how to recognise it, its templates and its problems.
- [Pattern drill](pattern-drill.md). You see only the problem and pick its pattern from 4 choices, in about 10 seconds a card.
- [New problems](new-problems.md). You add cards for problems that you did not attempt yet, on request or one each day.
- [Desk list](desk-list.md). You get a weekly list of the cards that you found hard, to code at a desk.
